Sync help

Preview an engine to inspect planned changes before applying them. Dry run does not change files; manual mode waits for review.

Storage checks guard new work. An online receiver can still have an unavailable share. Open the affected engine’s shared token instructions to check the marker files. Checks do not guarantee an in-progress transfer is protected from a later outage.

Receiver-only top-level directories are preserved, and sync does not delete directories. Eligible files within directories present on both servers may be deleted according to your approval policy.

Reliability grades reflect recorded engine successes and errors; they do not certify storage readiness or protect files.
